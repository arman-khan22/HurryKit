# Convert JPEG to JPG Online Privately

Standardize .jpeg image extensions to .jpg format directly in your browser without uploading files. Fast, private client-side conversion with batch processing.

## Why Convert JPEG to JPG?

Many legacy software applications, government forms, content management systems (CMS), and photo-printing kiosks still strictly require file names ending in `.jpg` rather than `.jpeg`. While the underlying image compression standard (ISO/IEC 10918-1) is exactly the same, file extension mismatches can trigger upload rejections or file associations errors on older operating systems.

Converting your JPEG files to JPG standardizes your image collection for universal cross-platform compatibility across Windows, macOS, Android, and web servers. Hurrykit accomplishes this instantly right inside your browser tab without transmitting your photos over the internet.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the difference between JPEG and JPG?
JPEG and JPG refer to the exact same image format. The 3-letter `.jpg` extension became common because early versions of Windows and MS-DOS enforced an 8.3 file naming convention that only allowed three-character extensions.

### Will converting JPEG to JPG reduce photo quality?
No. Standardizing from JPEG to JPG retains full visual quality. Hurrykit decodes and encodes the image locally with zero generational artifacting when quality is preserved.

### Are my photos uploaded to any server?
No. All conversions happen entirely in your web browser. Zero bytes leave your device.

### Can I convert multiple JPEG files at once?
Yes. You can batch convert up to 50 files simultaneously and download them individually or as a single ZIP archive.
