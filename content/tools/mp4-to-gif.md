# Convert MP4 Video Clip to Animated GIF Online

Turn MP4 video clips into looping animated GIFs right inside your browser. Perfect for social media, Slack reactions, memes, and email newsletters.

## Why Convert MP4 to GIF?

Short video clips are great, but many platforms—including GitHub readmes, email newsletters, Slack channels, Discord reaction slots, and forum signatures—do not allow embedded video players or require user click-to-play interactions. Animated GIFs play and loop automatically on every platform without requiring any video player controls or plugins.

Converting an MP4 clip into an animated GIF creates an instantly shareable visual snippet. Hurrykit optimizes color palettes using spatial and temporal dithering algorithms in WebAssembly, preventing color banding and keeping GIF file sizes manageable while preserving fluid motion.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drop your MP4 video clip into the converter interface.
2. Set your target frame rate (10 fps to 15 fps recommended for compact GIFs) and dimensions.
3. Click "Convert All" to generate the animated GIF frames in your browser.
4. Preview and download your looping animated GIF.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** MP4 video (H.264 / AAC)
- **Output Format:** Animated GIF (CompuServe GIF89a)
- **Palette Generation:** Adaptive 256-color palette with Floyd-Steinberg dithering
- **Processing:** 100% in-browser WebAssembly

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep your source clips short (under 10 seconds) to ensure your GIF file size remains compact for sharing.
- Choosing 12 to 15 frames per second provides smooth motion while saving significant file weight compared to 30 fps.
- Because all processing occurs in-browser, you can turn sensitive screen recordings into GIFs without uploading to cloud services.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I keep my GIF file size small?
Limit your clip duration to 3–6 seconds, reduce the resolution slightly, and use a frame rate of 10 to 15 fps.

### Does Hurrykit add watermarks to generated GIFs?
Never. Hurrykit is 100% free and places zero watermarks or branding on your converted animations.

### Are my video clips uploaded anywhere?
No. The GIF generation engine runs locally inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your videos remain completely private.

### Can I convert videos from my phone into GIFs?
Yes. Hurrykit works in mobile browsers on iOS and Android with no apps required.
