# Convert HEIC to JPG Online (Private & In-Browser)

Convert Apple HEIC photos into universally compatible JPGs instantly in your browser. Zero upload, complete privacy, batch downloading.

## Why Convert HEIC to JPG?

Apple devices capture photos in High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC) format by default to cut storage footprint in half while preserving 10-bit color data and depth maps. However, HEIC files frequently fail to open on Windows PCs without paid codec extensions, and they remain unsupported by many corporate portals, school submission systems, photo print kiosks, and older smart TVs.

Converting your HEIC images to standard JPEG format creates universal files that open instantly in every photo viewer, browser, operating system, and office productivity suite without requiring third-party software. Hurrykit handles the entire decoding and encoding process locally on your CPU via WebAssembly, guaranteeing zero server uploads and total privacy.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drag and drop your iPhone .heic or .heif photos into the converter above.
2. Adjust the output quality slider between 70% and 100% (default 90% delivers pristine visual fidelity with minimal file size).
3. Click "Convert All" to process all images directly in your browser.
4. Download your converted JPG images individually or package them into a single ZIP archive.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** HEIC / HEIF (Apple iOS 11+, macOS High Sierra+)
- **Output Format:** Standard JPEG / JPG (Baseline & Progressive)
- **Processing Engine:** In-browser libheif compiled to WebAssembly
- **Maximum Batch Size:** 50 photos per batch, up to 100 MB per file

## Tips & Best Practices

- For general photo sharing via messaging apps or email attachments, the 90% default quality provides an ideal balance of visual fidelity and compact size.
- Batch conversion works completely offline once the page has loaded in your browser window.
- EXIF orientation metadata is automatically parsed to ensure portrait and landscape photos remain correctly aligned.
- No data is uploaded to remote cloud servers, making this safe for private personal pictures and confidential documents.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does converting HEIC to JPG reduce photo quality?
Hurrykit uses a 90% quality default (fully adjustable up to 100%), ensuring virtually indistinguishable visual quality while making the image viewable on any device.

### Are my personal photos uploaded to any server?
No. All decoding and encoding happens 100% inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your photos never leave your device.

### Can I convert multiple iPhone HEIC photos at once?
Yes. You can select or drop up to 50 photos in a single batch and download them all individually or as a single convenient ZIP archive.

### Why won't my Windows PC open HEIC photos?
Windows does not include the HEIC/HEVC codec by default without a paid Microsoft Store add-on. Converting your photos to JPG solves this instantly with zero cost.
