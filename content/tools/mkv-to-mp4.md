# Convert MKV Video Container to Universal MP4 Online

Convert MKV video files into universal MP4 format with zero uploads. Play your videos smoothly on iPhone, iPad, Apple TV, and web browsers.

## Why Convert MKV to MP4?

Matroska Video (MKV) is a popular open-standard video container capable of holding unlimited video, audio, and subtitle tracks. However, Apple devices (iPhone, iPad, Mac QuickTime Player) and default web browsers refuse to play MKV containers natively, making it frustrating to watch downloaded videos, home movie backups, or drone recordings.

Converting MKV to MP4 remuxes or transcodes the media streams into the world's most widely supported multimedia container. Once converted to MP4, your video will play smoothly in QuickTime, Safari, iOS Photos, and any mobile media player. Hurrykit performs this conversion directly inside your browser using WebAssembly.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drag your .mkv video files into the upload window.
2. Select your output options for audio and video streams.
3. Click "Convert All" to process the video locally on your CPU.
4. Download your converted MP4 video file.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** MKV (Matroska Multimedia Container)
- **Output Format:** MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14 Container)
- **Audio Streams:** AAC Stereo encoding for maximum compatibility
- **Data Privacy:** Zero bytes transferred to external servers

## Tips & Best Practices

- Because MKV files often contain surround sound tracks (AC3/DTS) that mobile phones cannot play, Hurrykit downmixes audio to high-quality stereo AAC.
- Converting locally avoids the security risk of uploading personal video archives to unknown cloud conversion websites.
- Fast modern laptops with multi-core processors will transcode videos in just a fraction of their runtime.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why doesn't QuickTime or iPhone open MKV files?
Apple operating systems do not support the MKV container format natively. Converting the file to MP4 makes it immediately playable in QuickTime, Photos, and Safari.

### Does converting MKV to MP4 reduce video quality?
When using Hurrykit's default high-bitrate H.264 profile, image quality is maintained with virtually zero perceived loss in sharpness or color fidelity.

### Are my videos uploaded to any server during conversion?
No. All processing happens entirely inside your browser using WebAssembly. Your files remain 100% private on your machine.

### Can I convert MKV files with soft subtitles?
Hurrykit converts the primary video and audio streams into standard MP4. For hardcoded subtitles, ensure they are burned into the source video stream.
