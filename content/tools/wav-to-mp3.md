# Convert Studio WAV to MP3 Online at up to 320 kbps

Convert uncompressed WAV audio files into lightweight, universal MP3s. Encode at up to 320 kbps entirely in your browser with zero server uploads.

## Why Convert WAV to MP3?

WAV (Waveform Audio File Format) is the gold standard uncompressed format used in digital audio workstations (DAWs), podcast studio recorders, and musical production. While pristine in acoustic fidelity, 16-bit or 24-bit 44.1kHz stereo WAV files consume roughly 10MB of storage for every single minute of recorded sound (~50MB for a standard 5-minute song).

Converting WAV to MP3 applies perceptual psychoacoustic encoding to strip out frequencies imperceptible to human ears, slashing file sizes by up to 90% (from 50MB down to 5MB) while retaining sparkling audio clarity. This makes the files easy to email, upload to podcast hosting platforms, stream on websites, or play on mobile devices.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drag your .wav audio tracks into the converter window.
2. Choose your preferred MP3 bitrate: 128 kbps (speech), 192 kbps (balanced), or 320 kbps (studio fidelity).
3. Click "Convert All" to transcode the uncompressed PCM samples into MP3 frames.
4. Download your compact MP3 files.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** WAV / RIFF (16-bit, 24-bit PCM audio)
- **Output Format:** MP3 (CBR / VBR encoding up to 320 kbps)
- **Size Reduction:** Typically 85% to 90% smaller than raw WAV
- **Engine:** FFmpeg libmp3lame in WebAssembly

## Tips & Best Practices

- Select 320 kbps if you are distributing demo music tracks to listeners who value pristine sound reproduction.
- Use 192 kbps for podcast episodes to balance fast listener download times with natural voice timbre.
- Because conversion takes place on your device, even large 200MB WAV master files process rapidly without bandwidth delays.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What bitrate should I select for MP3?
192 kbps is the balanced default offering CD-like quality for general listening. For archival or professional production, choose 320 kbps. For speech recordings or audiobooks, 128 kbps provides smaller file sizes.

### Why convert WAV to MP3?
WAV files store raw uncompressed audio data (typically 10 MB per minute of audio). MP3 cuts file size by up to 90% while retaining high audio fidelity.

### Is my audio uploaded anywhere?
Never. All audio encoding runs locally on your computer or phone using an in-browser WebAssembly port of FFmpeg (libmp3lame).

### Does converting WAV to MP3 lose audio data?
MP3 is a lossy compression format, but at 256 or 320 kbps, the difference compared to uncompressed WAV is virtually indistinguishable to the human ear.
