# Convert AVIF to JPG Online for Universal Compatibility

Convert next-generation AVIF images to universal JPEG format in your browser. Fast, local decoding without sending files to the cloud.

## Why Convert AVIF to JPG?

AVIF (AV1 Image File Format) is an ultra-modern image compression format derived from the AV1 video codec. It offers remarkable compression efficiency, often beating WebP and JPEG by 50% or more. However, software adoption for AVIF is still fragmented: many desktop graphics editors, operating system file pickers, email clients, and social networks do not yet support .avif files.

When you download an AVIF photo from the web and find you cannot open it in Photoshop, attach it to a job application, or share it on Discord, converting to JPG is the quickest remedy. Hurrykit decodes AV1 video frames directly in WebAssembly and saves standard JPEG files that work everywhere.

## How to Convert

1. Drag and drop your .avif files into the conversion area.
2. Select your desired output quality (85%–95% recommended).
3. Click "Convert All" to transcode the AVIF images locally.
4. Download your universal JPG files instantly.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** AVIF (AV1 Image File Format)
- **Output Format:** Standard JPEG / JPG
- **Decoding Technology:** WebAssembly AV1 software decoder
- **Performance:** Fast client-side rendering with zero network delay

## Tips & Best Practices

- Keep quality at 90% or higher to preserve the rich color range and dynamic detail of high-quality AVIF photos.
- Batch conversion is fully supported: drop all your downloaded AVIF images to convert them in sequence.
- Because AV1 decoding is CPU intensive, modern multi-core devices will process batches significantly faster.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why can't I open AVIF images on my computer?
AVIF is a newer format based on the AV1 video codec. Many legacy photo viewers, office applications, and operating systems lack built-in AVIF codecs.

### Does converting AVIF to JPG reduce image quality?
Converting to high-quality JPG (90%+) retains virtually all visual detail while unlocking complete compatibility across all devices and software.

### Are my AVIF files uploaded to external servers?
No. Hurrykit processes all images locally inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your photos never touch a remote server.

### Can I convert animated AVIF files to JPG?
For animated AVIF sequences, Hurrykit converts the first keyframe into a high-resolution still JPG photo.
