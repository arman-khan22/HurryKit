# Convert WebP to PNG Online with Alpha Transparency

Convert WebP graphics to PNG format with full transparency preservation and zero loss of quality. In-browser processing with maximum privacy.

## Why Convert WebP to PNG?

WebP format is widely utilized across web pages for logos, transparent product renders, and website illustrations. However, when you download these images for use in digital art programs, vector editors, video editing suites, or design mockups, many desktop tools fail to recognize WebP files or strip their transparency channels.

Converting WebP to PNG ensures that transparent backgrounds, drop shadows, and subtle alpha gradients remain completely intact. PNG is the gold standard for lossless raster graphics across Adobe Creative Cloud, Affinity, Figma, Sketch, and Blender. Hurrykit decodes WebP alpha bitplanes accurately and encodes clean PNG chunks right inside your browser.
