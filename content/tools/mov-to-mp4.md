# Convert Apple MOV Video to MP4 Online

Convert iPhone and QuickTime MOV video clips to universal MP4 format in your browser. Eliminate playback errors with complete privacy.

## Why Convert MOV to MP4?

Apple iPhones, iPads, and Mac computers record video in QuickTime MOV container format by default. While MOV files play effortlessly on Apple devices, they frequently run into compatibility barriers when shared with Windows users, uploaded to web platforms, or played on Android phones, smart TVs, and video game consoles.

Converting Apple MOV videos to standard MP4 (MPEG-4 Part 14) with H.264 video and AAC audio ensures universal playback across every operating system, streaming platform, and digital device on the planet. Hurrykit executes this conversion directly in your browser using WebAssembly FFmpeg, protecting your personal video footage from cloud security risks.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drag your .mov video files into the drop zone above.
2. Select your desired video quality preset or leave on balanced default.
3. Click "Convert All" to start the local WebAssembly FFmpeg conversion.
4. Download your universal MP4 video files to your device.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** QuickTime MOV (Apple ProRes, H.264, HEVC)
- **Output Format:** MP4 (H.264 / AVC video with AAC audio)
- **Compatibility:** Universal across Windows, macOS, Android, iOS, Linux, Web
- **Processing Security:** Zero cloud uploads; 100% in-browser

## Tips & Best Practices

- If your MOV was shot on a modern iPhone in HDR, converting to standard H.264 MP4 ensures vivid colors render properly on standard dynamic range computer screens.
- Converting large video clips locally saves mobile data and avoids slow cloud upload times.
- Batch conversion lets you convert multiple camera clips in a single session.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why won't my Apple MOV video play on Windows?
Windows Media Player and older video apps lack native support for certain Apple MOV codecs and metadata tracks. Converting to MP4 solves this problem instantly.

### Does converting MOV to MP4 lower video quality?
Hurrykit uses high-grade H.264 encoding with visually lossless rate factors, ensuring your converted MP4 looks virtually identical to the original MOV.

### Are my private videos uploaded to external servers?
No. The entire conversion process runs inside your web browser via WebAssembly. Your videos never leave your computer.

### What is the maximum MOV file size I can convert?
You can convert video files up to 500 MB directly in your browser.
