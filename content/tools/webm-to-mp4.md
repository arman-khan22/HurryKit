# Convert WebM Video to MP4 Online for Any Player

Convert downloaded WebM videos into universal MP4 files in your browser. Compatible with all video editing software, mobile devices, and media players.

## Why Convert WebM to MP4?

WebM is an open media file format developed for HTML5 web video, commonly utilizing VP8 or VP9 video codecs with Vorbis or Opus audio. While web browsers like Chrome and Firefox excel at playing WebM, many video editing applications (such as Adobe Premiere Pro, Final Cut Pro, and DaVinci Resolve), default Windows video editors, and mobile gallery apps refuse to import or play WebM files.

Converting your WebM screen recordings, online video clips, and web downloads to MP4 restores universal compatibility. MP4 files import cleanly into all editing software and play without glitches on all iPhones, iPads, Android devices, and smart televisions. Hurrykit performs this transcoding directly in your browser without requiring server uploads.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** WebM (VP8, VP9, AV1 video with Opus/Vorbis audio)
- **Output Format:** MP4 (H.264 video with AAC audio)
- **Engine:** WebAssembly FFmpeg transcoding pipeline
- **Supported Size:** Up to 500 MB per video clip

## Tips & Best Practices

- Screen recordings captured in Chrome or Firefox are often saved as WebM. Converting to MP4 makes them easy to edit in desktop software.
- Because transcoding occurs in your browser, you never have to wait for large video files to upload to a remote server.
- Audio streams are converted to standard stereo AAC to guarantee playback on every sound system.
