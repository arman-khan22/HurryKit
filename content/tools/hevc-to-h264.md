# Convert HEVC (H.265) to H.264 Video Online

Transcode high-efficiency HEVC (H.265) videos to universal H.264 (AVC) in your browser. Fix black-screen playback errors on older PCs and TVs.

## Why Convert HEVC to H.264?

Modern smartphones (like recent iPhones and Samsung Galaxy devices), action cameras (GoPro), and drones capture 4K video using High Efficiency Video Coding (HEVC / H.265) to cut file sizes in half. However, HEVC playback requires specialized hardware decoders or paid operating system extensions. On older computers, Windows media players, web browsers, and smart TVs, HEVC videos often result in a black screen with audio only.

Converting HEVC to standard H.264 (Advanced Video Coding) restores full playback compatibility with virtually every screen and operating system manufactured in the last fifteen years. Hurrykit transcodes your video footage locally on your hardware using WebAssembly FFmpeg, ensuring fast processing and complete confidentiality.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Codec:** HEVC / H.265 (High Efficiency Video Coding)
- **Output Codec:** H.264 / AVC (Advanced Video Coding, Baseline & Main profiles)
- **Container:** Universal MP4 container
- **Processing Architecture:** In-browser WebAssembly CPU/Worker pipeline

## Tips & Best Practices

- Converting HEVC to H.264 eliminates the "Codec missing" or black screen error on Windows PCs.
- If you are uploading videos to web portals that reject H.265, H.264 is universally accepted.
- Because video re-encoding is computationally intensive, running on a plugged-in computer provides the fastest speeds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why does my HEVC video show a black screen with sound on Windows?
Windows 10 and 11 do not include the HEVC video codec out of the box. Converting the video to H.264 allows it to play natively in Windows Media Player and Movies & TV.

### Will converting from HEVC to H.264 increase the video file size?
Because H.264 is an older compression standard than H.265, maintaining the same visual quality may result in a slightly larger file size, but with 100% universal compatibility.

### Are my private video files uploaded to your servers?
No. All decoding and re-encoding executes entirely inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your files never leave your computer.

### Can I convert 4K GoPro and drone videos?
Yes, files up to 500 MB can be converted directly in your browser.
