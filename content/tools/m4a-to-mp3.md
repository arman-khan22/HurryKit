# Convert M4A Voice Memos to MP3 Online

Convert Apple Voice Memos and iTunes M4A audio files into universal MP3 format. Fast in-browser conversion with customizable bitrates and privacy.

## Why Convert M4A to MP3?

Apple iPhone Voice Memos, QuickTime audio recordings, and iTunes downloads are saved in MPEG-4 Audio (.m4a) containers encoded with AAC or ALAC. While Apple hardware plays M4A effortlessly, many older automotive USB stereos, non-Apple DJ software, podcast distribution tools, and transcription services still demand standard MP3 files.

Converting your M4A voice memos and song recordings to MP3 provides universal playback compatibility across every computer, Android smartphone, smart speaker, and media system. Hurrykit processes your audio tracks locally using FFmpeg WebAssembly, eliminating wait times and server privacy concerns.

## How to Convert

1. Select or drop your Apple .m4a audio files into the converter.
2. Select your desired MP3 bitrate (192 kbps standard, 320 kbps for audiophile fidelity).
3. Click "Convert All" to transcode the AAC audio streams locally.
4. Save your finished MP3 files to your local drive.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** M4A (MPEG-4 Audio with AAC or ALAC codec)
- **Output Format:** MP3 (MPEG Audio Layer III)
- **Processing:** In-browser WebAssembly FFmpeg worker
- **Max File Size:** Up to 500 MB per audio file

## Tips & Best Practices

- If you recorded a lecture, meeting, or interview on an iPhone Voice Memo, 192 kbps provides outstanding vocal presence.
- For music tracks recorded in lossless ALAC, select 320 kbps MP3 to preserve dynamic range and frequency separation.
- Conversions do not rely on server queues, so your files begin processing the instant you click convert.
