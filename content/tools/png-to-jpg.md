# Convert PNG to JPG Online with Custom Background

Convert heavy PNG images to lightweight JPG photos directly in your browser. Shrink file sizes by up to 80% with customizable background fill.

## Why Convert PNG to JPG?

PNG is the ideal format for logos and screenshots requiring transparency, but photographic PNG images often balloon in size to 10MB to 30MB or more. When uploading images to websites, attaching them to emails, or publishing them to digital storefronts, massive PNG files waste bandwidth and hurt page loading speed.

Converting PNG to JPG applies intelligent lossy compression to reduce file sizes by 70% to 90% while maintaining crisp photographic visual fidelity. Because JPEG does not support transparency, Hurrykit gives you full control over the background fill color (clean white by default, with black or custom tones available).

## How to Convert

1. Select or drop your PNG files into the converter area.
2. Set your target JPG quality slider (90% is the recommended sweet spot).
3. Choose your preferred background color for any transparent areas (default is clean white).
4. Click "Convert All" to process your files in milliseconds, then download the results.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** PNG (8-bit, 24-bit, 32-bit RGBA)
- **Output Format:** JPEG / JPG
- **Background Handling:** Automatic solid fill for alpha pixels (White, Black, Custom)
- **Size Reduction:** Typically 70% to 90% smaller

## Tips & Best Practices

- For screenshots of documents or websites, 85%–90% quality produces razor-sharp text with dramatically reduced file weight.
- Check the background color preview to ensure transparent logos blend smoothly into your chosen backdrop.
- Conversions process entirely inside your browser memory, guaranteeing speed and complete privacy.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why convert PNG to JPG?
PNG files are lossless and can be extremely large, especially for complex photos. Converting to JPG dramatically reduces file size (often by 70-90%) for faster web loading and easier email attachments.

### What happens to transparent backgrounds in PNG?
JPG does not support transparency. Hurrykit fills transparent areas with clean white by default, or you can select black or light grey in the options panel.

### Are any image files uploaded to Hurrykit servers?
Zero bytes leave your computer. The conversion is performed directly in your web browser using HTML5 Canvas and WebAssembly.

### Can I batch convert multiple PNG screenshots at once?
Yes, you can drop up to 50 PNG files at a time and download them individually or as a single ZIP archive.
