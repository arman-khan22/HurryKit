## Why Convert GIF to MP4?

The GIF format was created in 1987 and was never designed for modern high-resolution animation or long video playback. Because GIF encodes every frame as an uncompressed 256-color bitmap without inter-frame motion vector compression, even a short 5-second animation can easily weigh 10MB to 20MB.

Converting animated GIFs to modern MP4 (H.264) video typically shrinks the file footprint by 80% to 95% while dramatically improving playback smoothness and color fidelity. Furthermore, Instagram, TikTok, and many modern social media platforms do not allow uploading raw .gif files directly to feeds or stories, requiring MP4 video format instead. Hurrykit converts your GIFs to MP4 locally in seconds.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why is an MP4 video so much smaller than an animated GIF?
GIF stores full bitmap images for every frame, while MP4 uses advanced inter-frame compression that only stores differences between frames, slashing file size by up to 95%.
