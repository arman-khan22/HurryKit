# Convert Legacy AVI Video to Modern MP4 Online

Modernize legacy AVI video files into standard MP4 format directly in your browser. Play old home videos and camcorder clips on modern phones and tablets.

## Why Convert AVI to MP4?

AVI (Audio Video Interleave) was introduced by Microsoft in 1992 and was the standard format for video cameras, digital cameras, and PC multimedia for decades. However, AVI lacks support for modern streaming protocols, modern video compression algorithms, and mobile playback. Smartphones, tablets, smart TVs, and web browsers cannot play AVI files natively.

Converting your archival AVI home videos, digital camcorder clips, and security recordings to MP4 brings them into the modern era. MP4 offers dramatically superior compression efficiency and universal playback on every iPhone, Android device, Mac, and Windows PC. Hurrykit converts your AVI files locally on your computer with complete privacy.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** AVI (Audio Video Interleave with DivX, Xvid, or DV codecs)
- **Output Format:** MP4 (H.264 video with AAC audio)
- **Compression Gain:** Often 50% to 75% smaller file size with equal or better clarity
- **Privacy:** 100% client-side WebAssembly execution

## Tips & Best Practices

- Converting older camcorder AVI files to MP4 often cuts file size in half while making them easy to share via WhatsApp, Google Drive, or YouTube.
- Deinterlacing filters are automatically applied to interlaced camcorder footage to eliminate horizontal jagged lines.
- No files are transmitted to cloud servers, protecting precious family memories and private recordings.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Why won't my smartphone or tablet play AVI files?
AVI is a legacy 1990s container that modern mobile operating systems (iOS and Android) do not support natively. Converting to MP4 resolves this immediately.

### Will converting AVI to MP4 make the file size smaller?
Yes. Modern H.264 compression is vastly more efficient than older AVI codecs (like DivX or Cinepak), typically reducing file size by 50% or more.

### Are my private family videos kept secure?
Yes, 100%. The conversion runs locally inside your browser via WebAssembly. Your videos are never uploaded to the cloud.

### Can I convert multiple AVI clips at once?
Yes. You can add multiple clips to the queue and convert them sequentially in a single batch.
