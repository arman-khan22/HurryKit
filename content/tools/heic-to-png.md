# Convert HEIC to PNG Online with Transparent Support

Convert Apple HEIC photos into uncompressed, lossless PNG graphics directly in your browser. Perfect for design work, screenshots, and graphic editing.

## Why Convert HEIC to PNG?

While JPEG is ideal for standard photography, PNG (Portable Network Graphics) is the preferred standard when you need lossless fidelity, crisp line art, sharp text edges, or transparent background preservation. Converting Apple HEIC photos to PNG is especially valuable when working with iPhone screenshots, sticker cutouts, portrait mode cutouts with alpha channels, or assets destined for Photoshop, Figma, and Canva.

By converting directly to PNG, you eliminate the DCT block compression artifacts introduced by JPEG encoders. Hurrykit renders your HEIC images through high-precision WebAssembly decoders directly to 24-bit RGB or 32-bit RGBA pixel buffers, outputting clean, uncompressed PNG files with zero quality degradation.

## How to Convert

1. Drop your .heic or .heif files into the designated drop area above.
2. Review output options, including alpha channel retention and color depth.
3. Click "Convert All" to transform your files locally inside your browser.
4. Save your pristine PNG files individually or as a compressed batch ZIP.

## Technical Specifications & Details

- **Input Format:** HEIC, HEIF container
- **Output Format:** PNG (Portable Network Graphics, 24-bit / 32-bit RGBA)
- **Compression:** Lossless DEFLATE algorithm
- **Privacy Level:** 100% client-side WebAssembly execution

## Tips & Best Practices

- PNG files are lossless and therefore larger than JPGs. Use PNG when crisp text, sharp borders, or alpha transparency are essential.
- If you have cutout stickers created in iOS 16+, converting to PNG preserves their transparent backgrounds.
- All processing runs locally on your computer or phone hardware, meaning you do not consume internet upload bandwidth.

## Frequently Asked Questions

Yes. Hurrykit executes all processing 100% locally on your device via WebAssembly. Your files are never uploaded to any remote server.

### Does PNG retain higher quality than JPG?
Yes. PNG uses lossless compression, meaning no pixel data or sharp edges are discarded during encoding. This makes it superior for screenshots and graphics.
